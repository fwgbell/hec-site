# Product

**What:** Portfolio site for Hector Bell, a director and editor (music video, fashion, sport, commercials).

**Audience:** Commissioners, producers, agencies and production companies deciding whether to hire him. They usually arrive from a link in an email or on Instagram and give the site under a minute.

**Job:** Show the work immediately and let them play any film in one click. Directing and editing are separate bodies of work and get separate lists.

**Mode:** Experience. The work leads, and the interface recedes.

**Constraints:** Free hosting (Cloudflare Pages, static). Video only via Vimeo/YouTube embeds. Non-technical updates through one data file (`public/js/projects.js`).

**Brand commitments (from the client brief, `website-idea.pdf`):**
- Homepage: a handwritten-scan "Hector Bell" wordmark across the top of a full-bleed film slideshow, with small, neat tabs along the bottom: Directing, Editing, Info.
- Directing/Editing: a large typographic list of every project. Hovering a title fades the others and shows that project's stills.
- Every page except home has a small "hector–bell" logo in the corner. On hover the dash stretches (after the "new–land" reference).
- Info: a plain white page with portrait, short bio, links, and rep contact on the right.
